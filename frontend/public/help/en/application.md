Every offer that reaches the shortlist opens an application, and **Where this stands** on the offer detail is where you keep it. The app never sends anything, so every value here is what you say happened: the status, the day you sent it, the day to follow up, and a note.

<!-- screenshot: application-panel -->

Pick the **Application status** and press **Record**. An application starts at **New**; **Packaged** builds the application package, and it is the one step nothing may skip, because a sent application without a package would stand for a document nobody ever made. After that, no move is refused: **Sent**, **Replied**, **Interview**, **Offer**, and finally **Won**, **Lost**, **Rejected** or **Expired**, and a wrong one is corrected with one more record. **Sent on** and **Follow up on** feed the dashboard's follow-ups, and **History** lists every change with its day.

<!-- screenshot: cover-letter -->

The **Cover letter** is written when the package is built, in the language of the ad. A badge says who wrote it: a model draft, the template, or you. **Edit** opens it for changes and **Save letter** writes it into the package; **Draft again** asks for a new draft; **Copy** puts it on the clipboard with its formatting, ready for your mail client. Once an application is recorded as sent, the letter stays exactly as it went out.

The **Application package** is a folder on the machine that ran the run: the cover letter, the fixed CV for the ad's language and the reference projects that match. **Download package** bundles it as one archive.

**Tip:** set **Follow up on** in the same record as **Sent**. The dashboard counts what is due, and a closing status clears the reminder.
