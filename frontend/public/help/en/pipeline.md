This screen is the board of your applications, not the processing pipeline that reads the sources. Every offer that reaches the shortlist opens an application here, and the board groups them into five lanes: **Backlog**, **Prepared**, **Out**, **Talking** and **Closed**.

<!-- screenshot: pipeline-board -->

Move a card by dragging its grip onto another state, or pick the state on the card. Nothing is sent from the app, so everything on this board is what you record: when you sent it, when to follow up, and what came back. Moving an application to **Packaged** is what builds its package, which is why a card cannot skip that state on its way out.

**Tip:** set a follow-up date when you record an application as sent. The dashboard counts the follow-ups that are due, and a closing state clears the reminder for you.
