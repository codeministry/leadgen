Analytics shows what the market is doing, and what your rules are doing to it. The tiles at the top count the offers, the average per week, how many cleared the filter and how many of your sent applications were answered.

Below them, **Offers over time** can be read along the date an ad was published or the day it arrived, by day, week or month. The further sections show where the offers come from, the score distribution against the two thresholds, how many applications stand in each state, what the filter removes each week, and what each run did.

**Tip:** read the score distribution before you move a threshold. If most offers sit just below a line, the line is the question, not the market.
