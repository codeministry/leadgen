The row above the shortlist narrows and orders it. Everything you set there is part of the address, so the back button, a reload and a bookmark all return exactly this list.

<!-- screenshot: filters-panel -->

The score bands pick **All**, the offers at or above the shortlist line, the ones between the review line and the shortlist line, or those below the review line. **Filters** opens the rest: portals, whether an offer is scored at all, a score range, the start, a minimum duration, a topic from your profile, only offers whose deadline is still open, and possible duplicates. Every filter that is on shows as a chip under the row; a chip removes its filter, and **Clear all** removes them all. The number on **Filters** counts what is set behind it.

<!-- screenshot: sort-menu -->

The sort menu offers five orders, newest first when the list opens: highest score, newest first, starts soonest,
deadline nearest and longest duration. The toggle in front of it turns the current order round. In every order and in
both directions, an offer that states nothing about the field comes last, so it is never hidden.

**Views** saves the list as it stands under a name and brings it back with one click. A view lives in this browser only. **Find related** looks for offers that mean the same thing as your search in other words, and the chip it leaves takes you back. **Archive** at the top shows what left the working list; select several offers with their checkboxes to archive them at once, and every archived offer can be put back.

**Tip:** save the combinations you check every morning as views. It is quicker than setting four filters, and the view keeps the order too.
