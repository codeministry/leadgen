The offer detail is where you decide about one offer. It opens beside the shortlist or the pipeline board when you pick a card, and everything on it belongs to that one offer: the ad, its score, what was read out of it, and the application it opened.

<!-- screenshot: offer-why-scored -->

**The ad** is the advert as the portal published it, fetched once the offer survived the filter. Parts that belong to the portal rather than the offer (menus, cookie banners, "similar jobs") are folded away and named in a line above the text, so nothing is hidden without saying so. **Original ad** opens the portal's page. If the fetch failed, **Fetch the ad again** tries once more and shows why the last attempt did not work.

**Why it scored what it scored** lists every factor with the points it gave and the most it could give, and **Score again** judges this one offer anew when you have a reason the run could not know. **Extracted fields** shows what was read out of the ad: the source, the portal, the agency, the location, the remote share, the rate, the start, the duration and the deadline. A field the ad does not state says so rather than guessing. **Advertised by** names every agency that published the same offer, and a possible duplicate is marked so you do not apply twice.

<!-- screenshot: offer-ask -->

**Ask this advert** puts five fixed questions to the ad: the rate, the client, being on site, onboarding and an extension. Each answer comes from the ad alone and quotes the sentence it rests on. When the ad does not say, the answer says exactly that, and without a model, or before the ad was fetched, the panel explains why it cannot answer.

**Find related offers** searches for offers that mean the same thing in other words, and **Archive** takes this offer off the working list; the key **a** does the same.

**Tip:** read the quoted sentence, not only the answer. An ad that says "rate on request" is answered as silent, and that is the honest answer.
